About

Where technology meets design.

I'm a Computer Science student who enjoys the point where technology and design meet.

I'm studying at Holy Cross College and working toward a career as a Front-End Developer. What draws me to the front end is that it's where logic turns into something people can see, touch and use.

Outside of code, I'm interested in UI/UX, graphic design and basic video editing. Together they shape how I think about how a page looks, how it feels to use, and how clearly it communicates.
