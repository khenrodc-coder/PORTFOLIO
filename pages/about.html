About me

I'm Khen Rod S. Canlas, a Computer Science student at Holy Cross College. I enjoy developing websites, creating clean user interfaces, solving problems and exploring creative ways to bring ideas to life.

Introduction

Technology and design, with room to grow.

I'm driven by curiosity, creativity and a commitment to continuous growth. I like to look past what's expected, take on opportunities to learn, and turn ambitious ideas into useful, well-made things.

My studies in Computer Science give me the technical grounding behind the web. I build with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript and Tailwind CSS on the front end, and with Python, Flask and MySQL when a project needs a back end, as in SCMOVS and LLMS.

Alongside code, I work in UI design, graphic design and basic video editing. They shape how I think about layout, hierarchy and how clearly a page communicates.
